How this was made

  • Planet positions from Swiss Ephemeris, checked against NASA JPL DE440s: largest difference 6.1e-05°.
  • Chinese solar terms agreed across four engines to within 1.6 s; Four Pillars matched by two independent libraries.
  • 24 structural checks, 0 failures.
  • Birth time modelled as ±1 min (user-confirmed exact time, at most 1 minute off; ensemble uses +/-1 min (inner and outer identical)). No output changes inside that window.
  • Birthplace: Fatima General Hospital, Nishat Ganj / Mahanagar, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India (OpenStreetMap Nominatim).
  • Life-area rules were declared before comparing systems. Different reasonable rules would give different grades.
  • Maya and Tibetan meanings are left out because no verifiable source was available.
